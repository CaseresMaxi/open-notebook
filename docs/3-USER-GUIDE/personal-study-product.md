# Personal study product

This interface belongs to the personal fork. Its navigation focuses on **Sources, Notes, Summaries, Exams and Chat**, organized through notebooks.

- **Notebooks:** open a study workspace or create one. The tile/list switch and search work with existing notebooks.
- **Sources:** retain the reference library and source upload, extraction and processing behavior.
- **Notes:** choose a notebook and write or edit its notes. Saved chat answers remain ordinary notes.
- **Summaries:** choose a notebook and source, then generate a summary. The source must have extracted text, and a summary transformation and language model must be configured. Every saved summary links back to its original source and remains editable in its notebook.
- **Exams:** create, take and review exams with the existing question images, visual artifacts and answer grading.
- **Chat:** choose a notebook conversation. Context/memory controls, image attachments, inline visuals and interactive tests remain available.

On wide screens, a notebook displays sources, chat and a study panel for notes/summaries. On phones and narrower screens, use the tabs to switch panels. The mobile menu reaches all main destinations.

## Profile

Edit your display name, email and study description, then save. These values are stored only in this browser. They do not create an account, change your installation password or synchronize across devices. Appearance and language settings are also reachable here.

## Payments

Payments is a navigable product preview. It shows a personal installation, proposed plan categories, monthly/yearly selection, and empty payment method and invoice sections. Prices have not been defined. No payment provider is connected, no card details are collected, and no checkout or charges can occur.

## Configuration

Open **Settings** for processing preferences and the secondary links to models, transformations and advanced operational tools. Product navigation no longer exposes podcasts or other peripheral features as study destinations.
